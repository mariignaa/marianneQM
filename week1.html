Personal Observations

Most visitors mainly pass by to take pictures, while some international visitors use their phones to translate or search for information about the exhibits. Couples and groups typically spend 2–5 minutes interacting with the technology and discussing the exhibits together. Visitors who come alone tend to spend more time reading, exploring the interactive screens, and going through each section carefully. Most visitors only approach staff when they are particularly interested or curious about an exhibit.
